## Concrete implementation

- `scripts/e2e-local.sh --zones` runs the normal workspace seed, then the separate
  zone seed. The extracted seed stage exposes `E2E_ZONE_MANIFEST` only when opted
  in; existing `E2E_MANIFEST` and specs remain unchanged. Skip behavior applies
  independently to each manifest.
- The separate synthetic project has maps v1/v2, an empty site, two managers'
  revision-2 open drafts and 520 observations: 400 Standard, 80 Reliability,
  40 Inventory; 260 records per version. Map v1 includes a hole and a multipart
  zone. Map v2 shrinks and renames zone A, preserving its identity. V1 records
  arrive after v2 publication, with a point outside today's zone but inside its
  recorded polygon. The v1 label begins with `=` for export acceptance.
- Inventories bind their recorded package, zone and inventory form; upload bodies
  have no coordinates or first-round fields. Viewer reads verify null inventory
  geometry and all historical labels/package bindings through both result pages
  and the server summary.
- Stable request and observation IDs prevent duplicates. Interrupted publication
  resumes against immutable v1 geometry, checks the saved revision/note/content,
  and refuses other managers' changes. Publication closes revision 2 at revision 3.
  Open drafts are never silently replaced or overwritten on reruns.
- The existing browser seed now uses UUID bindings, nested answers, explicit
  inventory form binding and valid in-zone points. The public QGIS sample label
  is sent as the current import contract's `name`. Invalid imports are rejected;
  the seed no longer invents a persisted blocked package.
